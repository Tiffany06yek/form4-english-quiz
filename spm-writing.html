Question 1   Short email SPM 2024, Paper 2, Part 1 · about 80 words · 20 marks

SPM 2024, Paper 2, Part 1

You must answer this question.

You received an email from your friend, Ben, who is planning to buy a new watch.

Hi there,

I think it’s time I got myself a new watch. I know you just bought a new one. Why did you decide to buy it? How much did you pay for it? Do you think it’s better to buy the watch online or from a shop? Why?

Bye!

Ben

Write an email to your friend in about 80 words in the space provided.

To:ben@email.com
Subject:A new watch

Writing outline

Notes only — join them into your own sentences. Do not copy a whole answer.

Greeting

  • Hi Ben, / Hey Ben,

Opening

  • thanks / your email / glad you asked
  • Thanks for… / I’m glad…

Why you bought it

  • old watch – stopped working / always late
  • or: waterproof / look neat for school
  • I decided to buy it because…

Price

  • RM ___ / sale / last month
  • I paid… / It cost me…

Online or shop + why

  • shop – try it on / see the quality
  • or: online – cheaper / more choices
  • short reason; optional: 1 small example
  • I think it is better to buy it… because…

Closing

  • Hope this helps. / That’s all from me.
  • Bye! / Write back soon.

Word count: 0   (aim for about 80 words)

Question 2   Short email SPM 2022, Paper 2, Part 1 · about 80 words · 20 marks

SPM 2022, Paper 2, Part 1

You must answer this question.

You received an email from your friend, Diana who plans to help a classmate, Julia move into her new house.

Hi,

Julia and her family are moving into their new house this Sunday. We should lend them a hand. Let’s go there together. Where should we meet and what time works best for you? I’m still thinking about how we can help them. Any ideas?

Should we bring anything?

Let me know what you think.

Diana

Write an email to your friend in about 80 words in the space provided.

To:diana@email.com
Subject:Let’s help Julia

Writing outline

Notes only — join them into your own sentences. Do not copy a whole answer.

Greeting

  • Hi Diana,

Opening

  • agree to help / this Sunday
  • Yes, let’s help Julia…

Where and what time

  • place + time – school gate / her house / 8.30 a.m.
  • We can meet at… at…

How to help

  • 2 simple jobs – carry boxes / wipe windows / pack / arrange furniture
  • I think we can… / We could also…

What to bring

  • 1–2 things – newspapers / cleaning cloth / drinks / kuih
  • We should bring…

Closing

  • See you on Sunday! / Let me know if this works.

Word count: 0   (aim for about 80 words)

Question 3   Guided essay SPM 2024, Paper 2, Part 2 · 125–150 words · 20 marks

SPM 2024, Paper 2, Part 2

You must answer this question.

Your class has been talking about music and its influence on teenagers. Your teacher has asked you to write an essay about it.

In your essay, you should:

  • tell which type of music you like
  • say why you like that type of music
  • explain how important music is in your life

Use all the notes above and give reasons for your point of view.

Write your essay in 125 – 150 words in an appropriate style.

Writing outline

Notes only — join them into your own sentences. Use all three points.

Intro

  • topic + the type you like
  • pop / R&B / rap / classical / Chinese songs / local singers
  • Many teenagers… / The type of music I like is…

Why you like it

  • 2 reasons – easy to remember / lyrics / mood / after school
  • I like this type of music because… / It also…

How important in your life

  • 2 everyday examples – revise / bus / kitchen / before a test
  • Music is important in my life because… / I listen to it when…

Conclusion

  • short wrap-up – no new point
  • All in all,… / Without music,…

Word count: 0   (aim for 125–150 words)

Question 4   Guided essay SPM 2021, Paper 2, Part 2 · 125–150 words · 20 marks

SPM 2021, Paper 2, Part 2

You must answer this question.

Your class has been discussing the ways people can show kindness to others and your teacher has asked you to write an essay on it.

In your essay, you should write about:

  • an act of kindness you experienced
  • why we must be kind to others
  • how to encourage people to be kind

Use all the notes above and give reasons for your point of view.

Write your essay in 125 – 150 words in an appropriate style.

Writing outline

Notes only — join them into your own sentences. Use all three points.

Intro + the act of kindness

  • one short story – who / what / result
  • rain, umbrella, senior, bus stop / or your own story
  • An act of kindness I experienced was… / Last year,…

Why we must be kind

  • 2 reasons – bad day / small action / comes back / warmer school
  • We must be kind because… / Kindness also…

How to encourage people

  • 2 practical ideas – kindness week / thank people out loud / notice good deeds
  • To encourage people to be kind,… / We can also…

Conclusion

  • short close – everyone can / simple ways
  • In the end,… / Kindness does not have to be…

Word count: 0   (aim for 125–150 words)
