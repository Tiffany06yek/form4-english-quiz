PART A   Tenses Questions 1–8 · Multiple choice · 8 marks

Choose the most suitable answer.

1. By the time we arrived at the hall, the briefing ________.

2. My brother ________ in Johor Bahru since 2022.

3. Listen! The students ________ the school song in the hall.

4. If she ________ harder, she will pass the test.

5. We ________ to Penang last holiday and visited our grandparents.

6. This time tomorrow, we ________ on the school bus to the museum.

7. I ________ my keys. I cannot find them anywhere.

8. While the teacher ________ the poem, someone knocked on the door.

PART B   Subject–verb agreement Questions 9–13 · Fill in the blanks · 5 marks

Fill in each blank with the correct form of the verb in brackets. Write one word only.

9. Neither of the boys ________ (want) to leave early.

10. The news from the camp ________ (be) encouraging, so everyone is excited.

11. Each of the students ________ (have) a workbook.

12. My friends, as well as my cousin, ________ (be) coming to the study session.

13. Ten kilometres ________ (be) quite a long way to walk.

PART C   Prepositions Questions 14–17 · Fill in the blanks · 4 marks

Fill in each blank with a suitable preposition. Write one word only.

14. She is interested ________ joining the debate club.

15. We have been waiting ________ the bus for twenty minutes.

16. The teacher congratulated him ________ his excellent essay.

17. I prefer reading ________ watching television.

PART D   Articles Questions 18–21 · Fill in the blanks · 4 marks

Fill in each blank with a, an, the, or — (write a dash if no article is needed).

18. He wants to be ________ engineer when he finishes school.

19. ________ Mount Kinabalu is the highest mountain in Malaysia.

20. We had ________ unexpected holiday last week because of the flood.

21. Please close ________ door before you leave the classroom.

PART E   Error identification Questions 22–24 · Multiple choice · 3 marks

Each sentence contains one error. Choose the underlined part (A–D) that is incorrect.

22. The numberA of students who isB takingC Additional Mathematics has increasedD.

23. She did not wentA to schoolB yesterday becauseC she was unwellD.

24. Despite ofA the heavy rainB, the football matchC continuedD.

PART F   Mini writing — Short email SPM Paper 2 Part 1 style · 80–100 words · Marked by your teacher

You have received the email below from your friend, Daniel, who has just started Form 4. Write an email to Daniel in 80–100 words.

Email from Daniel

Hi,

I’ve just started Form 4 and everything feels so different! There are so many subjects and I’m not sure how to organise my time. Any advice? Also, should I join a club?

Write back soon.

Daniel

In your email, you should:

  • tell him how you felt when you started Form 4
  • give him two useful tips for doing well this year
  • suggest one after-school activity he might enjoy
  • invite him to a study session this weekend
